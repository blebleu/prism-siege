    Treasure Past

    How to play

    Survive wave after wave of neon prisms. Every enemy you break drops gold shards: each one raises your score multiplier by 1. Dying resets the multiplier, so stay alive to score big.

    Keyboard & mouse

    • WASD move
    • Mouse aims (fires automatically; hold click if auto-fire is off)
    • ←↑↓→ aim and fire without a mouse
    • Space or right-click: dash (you can't be hurt mid-dash)
    • E or Q: bomb
    • Esc or P: pause

    Gamepad

    • Left stick moves, right stick aims and fires
    • A / RB: dash · B / LB: bomb · Start: pause

    Touch

    • Drag on the left half to move, on the right half to aim and fire
    • Dash and Bomb buttons sit in the corner

    Know your enemy

    • Mote drifts about aimlessly
    • Seeker homes straight in
    • Dodger side-steps your bullets
    • Splitter tough; bursts into three
    • Charger aims, flashes, then rams
    • Spitter keeps away and fires orbs (shoot them down)
    • Warden the boss, every 8th wave

    Power-ups (Spread, Rapid, Pierce) last 12 seconds. Extra lives and bombs come with score. Enemies flicker in before they can hurt you.
